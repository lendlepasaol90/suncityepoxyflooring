Local floor coating specialists

Floors chosen for how you actually use the space.

Sun City homes have a rhythm of their own. The garage holds the car, the golf cart and often a workbench or a freezer. The patio is where morning coffee happens from October through April. The laundry room and the Arizona room see steady, everyday use. Each of those spaces deserves a floor that looks tidy, cleans quickly and does not ask for much attention.

That is what a well-installed coating does. We start by grinding the concrete to remove old paint, sealers and the dusty top layer, repair cracks and pitted spots, and test for moisture. From there we tailor the layers to each space — speckled flake in the garage, a light, grippy finish outdoors, a soft satin color inside the house. The result is a sealed surface that wipes clean, resists stains and holds up to daily traffic for years.

We keep the process calm and predictable. You get a clear written estimate, a realistic schedule, and a crew that protects your home and tidies up at the end of each day.

Garage epoxy flooring

A garage floor that keeps up with the car, the cart and the workbench.

Many garages around here do double duty. One bay holds the car; the other is home to a golf cart, a charger, a second freezer and a row of storage cabinets. Bare concrete in that setup gets scuffed by cart tires, stained by drips and dusty no matter how often it is swept.

A full-broadcast flake floor sealed with a polyaspartic topcoat solves most of that. The speckle camouflages dust, the surface offers steady footing, and the clear top layer shrugs off warm tires and stays color-true even where the late-day sun pours in. We can also coat the short stem walls so the whole space looks finished.

Patios, pool decks & walkways

Make the patio the most comfortable room of the house.

In the cooler months, the patio becomes an extra living room. But sun-faded, cracked or stained concrete can make it feel neglected. A coating gives the slab a fresh, even finish in a color that complements your stucco and furniture, and it seals out grill grease, leaf stains and hard-water marks from the sprinklers.

Outdoors we choose UV-stable materials that won’t yellow, and we build texture into the top layer so the surface grips when it is wet from pool splash or a monsoon shower. Lighter shades stay more comfortable underfoot in the heat. We also pay attention to the joints and edges, so the coating looks clean where it meets the house, the lawn or the pool coping.

Residential, decorative & metallic

Three looks, endless combinations.

Most interior and garage floors start with one of these finishes. Choose a style below to see how it works.

Decorative flake flooring

Colored vinyl flakes are scattered into the base coat and sealed under a clear topcoat. The speckled finish hides dust and small marks remarkably well, and the subtle texture adds grip. Blends range from quiet grays and taupes to warmer mixes that echo desert landscaping. It is our most popular finish for garages and works beautifully in laundry rooms and workshops too.

Flake colors and options

Residential epoxy flooring

A single, seamless color with a satin or gloss sheen gives interior rooms a clean, finished look with no grout lines to scrub. It suits laundry rooms, mudrooms, craft rooms and Arizona rooms where you want a durable floor that looks calm rather than busy. A whisper of grit can be mixed in for traction, and a short coved edge along the walls makes mopping corners effortless.

Interior floor coatings

Metallic epoxy flooring

Pearlescent pigments are worked by hand into the wet resin, creating soft, flowing patterns that resemble marble or polished stone. Every floor is one of a kind. They make a beautiful statement in a den, foyer, bar nook or craft room, and a satin top layer keeps reflections gentle.

Installation process

What to expect, step by step.

Most garages take one to two days. Larger or more involved projects take a little longer.

  1. Step 1

    A visit and a written plan

    We look at the floor together, check for moisture and old coatings, measure, and show you color samples in your own light. Afterward you receive a written estimate listing the prep work, any repairs and each coat.

  2. Step 2

    Grinding and repairs

    On installation day we protect the surrounding area and grind the concrete with vacuum-equipped equipment. Cracks, chips and low spots are filled and smoothed so they disappear under the finish.

  3. Step 3

    Base coat and color

    A base coat is applied to bond with the concrete. For flake floors, the flakes are broadcast by hand while the base is wet; for solid or metallic floors, the color layer is built and finished.

  4. Step 4

    Protective topcoat

    After any loose flake is scraped and vacuumed away, a clear topcoat seals the floor. If you’d like extra grip, a fine texture is added at this stage.

  5. Step 5

    Walkthrough and care tips

    We walk the finished floor with you, confirm when you can walk, park and move items back, and leave written care instructions.

Benefits of professional epoxy flooring

Less upkeep, more peace of mind.

A professionally installed coating changes the day-to-day experience of a space. Here is what our customers tend to appreciate most.

  • Sweeps clean in minutes. Dust, leaves and grass clippings sit on top of a sealed floor instead of working into the concrete.
  • Resists stains. Oil, fertilizer, coffee and grill drips wipe up rather than leaving permanent marks.
  • Better footing. Textured flake and grit additives help prevent slips — especially at entries and on wet patios.
  • A brighter garage. Light, reflective floors make it easier to see what you’re doing at the workbench or in the evening.
  • No more concrete dust. Sealing the slab stops the fine gray powder that settles on everything stored nearby.
  • Long-lasting. A properly bonded system holds up for many years, and the topcoat can be refreshed rather than replaced.
  • A finished look. A coated garage or patio makes the whole home feel cared for — a nice touch if you ever decide to sell.

Local flooring considerations

A few things we keep in mind in Sun City.

Established concrete

Much of Sun City was built from the 1960s onward, so many garage slabs have decades of history: old paint, worn sealers, settled cracks and oil soaked deep into the surface. These floors coat beautifully, but they need thorough grinding and a primer chosen for the conditions. We test before quoting so there are no surprises.

Serious summer heat

Low-desert summers regularly push well past 100 degrees, and a closed garage gets hotter still. Heat speeds up how quickly coatings set, so in summer we start early, mix in smaller batches and select products formulated for warm slabs. Topcoats are UV-stable so colors stay true near the door.

Golf carts and charging

Carts turn sharply on the same patch of floor every day, and chargers can drip. We build extra durability into that bay and make sure the topcoat is resistant to the chemicals involved.

Dust storms and monsoon rain

Summer storms push fine dust under garage doors and drop sudden rain on patios. Sealed, textured surfaces sweep and rinse clean, and keep their grip when wet.

Keeping disruption low

We know your home is where you spend your day. Fast-cure systems, lower-odor products and tidy work habits keep the inconvenience to a minimum.

Frequently asked questions

Answers before you ask.

Can a garage coating handle a golf cart and a car side by side?

Yes. Golf carts are light compared with a car, but their small tires turn tightly in the same spots every day. A full flake system with a polyaspartic topcoat handles that easily. If the cart charges in the garage, we also make sure the area around the charger is sealed so any battery drips stay on the surface.

How long will I be without my garage?

With our one-day polyaspartic systems, most two-car garages are finished in a single visit. You can walk on the floor the following morning, and cars and carts can usually return within a few days, depending on the weather. We confirm exact times before we leave.

Is there a strong smell?

Every coating has some odor while it is being applied. For attached garages and interior rooms we use lower-odor products where they suit the job, keep the space ventilated and close the door to the house. Most people find the smell has faded by the next day.

My home was built decades ago. Can the slab still be coated?

Almost always. Older slabs often have paint, sealers or decades of oil to deal with, and some lack a vapor barrier underneath. We grind off old finishes, test for moisture and choose a primer to suit what we find. Age alone is not a problem — preparation is what matters.

Will the floor be slippery?

Not if it is built correctly for the space. Flake floors have a natural texture, and we can add a fine grit to the topcoat for extra grip. For patios, pool decks and entries we always recommend a textured finish.

Do I need to be home during the work?

Someone needs to let us in on the first morning and see the finished floor at the end. Many customers go about their day in between. For garage work, you will want to park on the driveway or street until the floor is ready.

What does a coated patio feel like in summer?

Lighter colors stay noticeably cooler than dark ones, and a textured finish is comfortable underfoot. Nothing stays cool in full July sun, so we match the shade to how exposed your patio really is.

How do I clean the floor afterward?

A dust mop or soft broom handles most days. When it needs more than a sweep, a bucket of warm water, a splash of neutral cleaner and a soft mop will do. We leave simple written care instructions with every floor.
