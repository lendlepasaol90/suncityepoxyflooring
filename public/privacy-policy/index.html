Privacy Policy

Sun City Epoxy Flooring respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

Submitting the estimate form sends us the details you type in — your name, phone, email, project type and optional note — plus the address of the page you were on.

How we use it

Those details are used solely to reply to you, arrange a visit and keep in touch about the work. We never sell or rent your information.

Third-party services

Our website uses Google Maps and Google Fonts, which may collect usage information under Google’s privacy policy. Form submissions are processed by our website host.

Questions

If you’d like to review or delete what we hold, phone (623) 000-0000 or visit our contact page.
