Service areas

Proudly serving Sun City and the West Valley.

We focus on Sun City and nearby communities so we can offer prompt estimates, convenient scheduling and attentive service.

Sun City

Sun City is where most of our work happens. We coat garages, golf cart bays, patios, Arizona rooms and laundry rooms throughout the community, on both sides of Grand Avenue. Many homes here were built from the 1960s onward, and we’re experienced with the older slabs, painted floors and settled cracks that come with that history.

Sun City West

In Sun City West, extended garages with room for a golf cart are common, as are covered patios that see daily use for much of the year. Flake floors with polyaspartic topcoats and light, textured patio coatings are our most requested projects here.

Youngtown

Youngtown sits right on Sun City’s eastern edge. We coat garages and patios in its established neighborhoods and help small businesses along the main corridors with commercial floors.

Peoria

Peoria neighborhoods near Sun City range from older homes to newer subdivisions. Newer slabs are often in good shape and coat quickly; older ones may need a little more preparation.

Surprise

In Surprise, including Sun City Grand and the communities along Bell Road, we coat garages, cart bays, patios and entries. Larger garages and three-car layouts are common here.

El Mirage

Just west of Sun City, El Mirage includes both homes and light-industrial properties. We handle garage coatings as well as shop and warehouse floors.

Glendale (west)

We serve west Glendale neighborhoods for garage and patio projects and take on commercial floors in nearby retail and office spaces.

Arrowhead area

Around Arrowhead and the Loop 101 corridor, we coat residential garages and patios and help retail, medical and service businesses with commercial flooring.

Not on the list?

If your home or business is nearby but not listed, please get in touch. We regularly help customers just beyond these communities, especially for garage and commercial projects. Every service we offer — from garage coatings and patio coatings to commercial floors — is available throughout our service area.

Questions about a floor? We’re happy to talk it through.
